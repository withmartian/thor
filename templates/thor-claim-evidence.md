# Claim-Evidence Map (thor-claim-evidence.md)

Read role and rules: [Claim-Evidence Map](thor-document-rules.md#claim-evidence-map).

Record durable empirical claims and their supporting, weakening or narrowing
evidence. Update an existing claim when new evidence changes its scope or support;
add a claim only for a genuinely new insight. Keep fewer claims than experiments.
Organize by claim; put chronology in `thor-results-by-time.md` and speculation in
conjecture files. Apply the active contract's review policy before promoting evidence.

Confidence labels:

- **High**: repeated across conditions, with a direct artifact trail.
- **Medium**: supported but interpretation-, assay-, or replication-dependent.
- **Low**: suggestive, not yet stable.
