# Claim-Evidence Map (thor-claim-evidence.md)

Durable empirical claims and the evidence that supports, weakens, or narrows
them. Keep fewer claims than experiments. Do not organize by chronology (that is
`thor-results-by-time.md`). Keep conjecture out of this file.

Confidence labels:

- **High**: repeated across conditions, with a direct artifact trail.
- **Medium**: supported but interpretation-, assay-, or replication-dependent.
- **Low**: suggestive, not yet stable.
