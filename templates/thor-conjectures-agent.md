# Agent Conjectures (thor-conjectures-agent.md)

Read role and rules: [Conjectures](thor-document-rules.md#conjectures).

Agent-owned. Record your beliefs, disagreements with `thor-conjectures-human.md`,
and proposed alternatives here. For empirical revisions, record and score the
evidence in the study note first, then apply the active contract's review policy
before updating beliefs. Literature- or reasoning-led ideas state that origin;
reflection alone is not an empirical result or a skeptic review.

Phrase entries as beliefs, predictions, falsifiers, and alternatives. Link to
results docs as backlinks rather than restating results here.
