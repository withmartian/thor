# Human Conjectures (thor-conjectures-human.md)

Read role and rules: [Conjectures](thor-document-rules.md#conjectures).

Human-owned. Agents read these as hypotheses and leave edits to the human unless
explicitly authorized. Record agent disagreement and proposed revisions in
`thor-conjectures-agent.md` instead. A generic instruction to update conjectures
does not grant ownership of this file.

Human: state beliefs plainly, including ones the agent may be unaware of. Expect
them to be wrong and to change over time — that is the point. Link empirical
revisions to evidence under the project's review policy.

Phrase entries as beliefs, predictions, falsifiers, and alternatives — not as
"we observed". Observations belong in the results docs.
